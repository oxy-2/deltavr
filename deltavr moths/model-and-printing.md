the shell is half the product. deltavr moths have to look like something worth buying, survive sweat, and stay flat.

## shell concept

- smoothed gray asa outer
- expanding lw-tpu flex middle so the butterfly wings can sit and wires can run for touch and battery
- thin clear petg window over the enig art
- bottom is smoother black asa (or just smooth gray, we had both in the chat)
- transparent top is the dream, clear asa does not actually do that. clear petg or clear pc does

## materials

| part | material | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| outer shell | gray asa | acetone vapor smooths to gloss, survives handling |
| flex middle | expanding lw-tpu | keeps the fold flexible, routes wires |
| window | thin clear petg | shows the gold, chemically fine against acetone |
| optional inner foam | soft foam under board | kills heel-stomp vibes before they hit the imu |

no carbon fiber or conductive filaments anywhere near the antenna. those cage the radio.

## the finish (this is the "18 year old in madeira" flex)

1. print the gray asa shell
2. acetone vapor smooth once. it goes glossy, layer lines flatten
3. let it outgas and re-harden a full day
4. slot the clear petg window into its place
5. thermal weld the asa edge over the petg lip with a soldering iron, low temp around 230 to 250c, chisel tip
6. do **not** vapor smooth a second time over the weld. stressed seam will pull away and open holes
7. instead: light scrape / 400 grit on the weld bump, then a q-tip of liquid acetone right on the seam only
8. clip-on back so its repairable and sweat safe

exposed pcb gets clear conformal coat before the shell closes. see [[hardware]].

## print cost

from the butterfly pricing chat, a 10-tracker case set plus straps is roughly 400 to 600g of filament. about 10 to 15 usd per set in pla/petg, more if we are actually printing asa and lw-tpu. electricity is noise. failures and reprints are the real line item, especially while the shell is still moving.

strap and mount hardware is another 15 to 25 a set if we include it in the box.
