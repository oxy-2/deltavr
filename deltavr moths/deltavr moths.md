deltavr moths are the sellable trackers. part of deltavr, not a side quest. smolslime class body trackers on nrf52840 (nice!nano style pro micro) with our own butterfly-fold pcb so we can actually build and ship them. similar idea to chrysalis butterflies, except oxy designed the boards and we assemble them ourselves. fully open source, enig gold, a bit too pretty for the price.

goal is simple: sell finished sets so the headset stops eating the whole budget. even 200 eur for a set of 8 fully built, make two sets, get 400 back, and the headset nets out at something like 100 to 200. thats insane for what it is.

raw gemini chats live in the `source/` folder. notes split out like this:

- [[hardware]]: pcb, touch, antenna, the butterfly fold
- [[software]]: slimevr side and what buyers actually run
- [[firmware]]: nrf, imu tap, esb radio, battery life
- [[model-and-printing]]: shell, flex middle, vapor smoothing, the window
- [[profit-and-expense]]: set pricing vs official butterflies, what we actually keep

pricing anchor from the chats: two sets of 10 at $450 each is $900 gross against about $200 in parts. official slimevr butterfly full-body set is $449 for 10 + 1 dongle. we are in the same band and we ship finished.
