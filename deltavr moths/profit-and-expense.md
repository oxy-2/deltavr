so the honest take is closer to 500 than 700 once you sell on etsy/ebay instead of hand-to-hand cash.

fee details if we go online:

- etsy / ebay class fees roughly 6.5 to 13 percent (about 30 to 60 a set)
- paypal goods and services or stripe around 2.9 percent plus 30 cents (~13.50 a set)
- boxes, wrap, tape about 3 to 5 an order

local cash skips most of that. online scales and costs.
