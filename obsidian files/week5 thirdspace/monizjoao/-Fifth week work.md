The next lapse I made was of researching how SLAM works and it taught me a lot on how it works. From my understanding, the software uses the texture on frames to map out feature points on a map. Then the IMUs calculate the inertial values to find the speed, acceleration and that the trackers were on and for how long. This determines the position the trackers are in on a 3D scale and at the same time makes the map of the room with the cameras (kind of like lidar). Now this isn't the definitive truth and it's just my understanding of it. It's also very summarized so for more information check the research file [here](https://github.com/oxy-2/deltavr/blob/main/Research/Research_ORB-SLAM3.txt).
