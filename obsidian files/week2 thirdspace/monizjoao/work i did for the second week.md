This second week me and Oxy focused on modeling the IPD stuff for the headset and also how the displays and lenses are gonna be.
Firstly we did a rack and pinion system as a prototype although this was changed later.
![[Pasted image 20260927151430.png]]
(Early prototype of the rack and pinion system.)
![[Pasted image 20260927151546.png]]
(Assembly simulation of the rack and pinion system.)
Lapse for the rack and pinion system: https://lapse.hackclub.com/timelapse/Yn8y69aQsNER

This system was ok for what we were gonna do but it was hard to model and actually implement so we decided to change it to a sliding assembly with metal rods and a display plate.
This system wont doesn't do anything as of right now but eventually we'll make it work.
![[Pasted image 20260927151949.png]](Rod system we modeled out.)
Lapse for the remade system: https://lapse.hackclub.com/timelapse/JYO1MD31HEyD

Modeling the rod system consisted of figuring out the measurement of the rod itself and sticking it into the headset on the right spot before modeling the end caps.
Then we figured we'd use small holders to hold the displays.
![[Pasted image 20260927152620.png]](Early holders for the displays)
Lapse for the early holders: https://lapse.hackclub.com/timelapse/zojeA41YhIfv

To hold the displays I modeled the holders to have a flush fit to the displays as kind of like slots but that didn't work so Oxy asked me to model a dovetail for the holders which we also ended up not using.
![[Pasted image 20260927153111.png]]
(Unused dovetail model)

At the end we decided to actually use some sort of adhesive for the displays so we made a much simpler slot system that consisted of four long cylinders with a plate between them and clearance of 0.6mm between them and the rod so they can slide around. 
![[Pasted image 20260927153350.png]]
(New display holder system)

After finishing this we decided to finally address the elephant in the room and fix all the errors the 3d model had. Approximately one third of the parts we had on the project were not generating because of errors. 

Firstly we made a new branch for the project in case we messed anything up. After that we got to work and deleted a bunch of parts that had generation errors.
We also used that time to delete a bunch of transforms we had that were essentially clogging the program up with bloat that was unnecessary.

During this whole IPD system remake we had the headset hidden to be able to work on the mechanics without having the headset blocking our view. Unfortunately when we reopened the file it so happened that the holders clipped through the headset which was a problem. For this reason we took another hour to make bumps on the outside of the headset in order for the holders to fit.
