On Friday I worked on the strap mechanism. the concept was that the strap was made of a flexible material and the rack and pinion system made it move forward and back in such a way that it tightens the strap into the skull.

I also made an assembly simulation for it so I can see if it works or not. This however was broken at the time and didn't show me much on the success of the system.
