Modeling :
As i was getting sick of spending my days slaving away my days writing virtual hardware that i basically don't understand and doesn't exactly work, i asked my team leader Rhime if there is any modelling task that i could help with. Luckily there was, the task was to make a holder for the headsets cameras.

![[Screenshot 2026-10-11 at 18.13.38.png]]

I had not done any work in cad software before so i tried finding a small course to get acquainted with the software. I was unsuccessful at this and decided to "wing it".
The work wasn't that hard when i started, Rhime gave me a short explanation on how to add sketches and use extrude, witch were the only tools i really used and everything else was fairly self explanatory. 

I came up with a simple design that hugs the camera letting it slide in from the top and left space for two screw holes on the bottom and then sent it over to Rhime to see his opinion.

![[Screenshot 2026-10-11 at 13.54.31.png]]
![[Screenshot 2026-10-11 at 09.24.40.jpg]]
![[Screenshot 2026-10-11 at 09.24.28.jpg]]

He said it would probably be fine so i continued on working, though making the part that holds the camera itself was easy but a little slow due ti my lack of experience, making a supporting structure to link it to the main body of the headset was a challenge, i couldn't get the angles right, not have it intersecting crucial components and other problems were making it quite the headache but i persevered and made a single leg connection that i was more or less sure would be good enough.

Unfortunately i do not have a picture of this design, but as it turns out my other teammate Monizjoao was also working on the same task, and after we compared then we decided that mine was better, although not great. So we upgraded it and came up with this.
