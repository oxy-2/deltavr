After doing the rack itself i worked on making an axel and I'm pretty proud of how I did for the first time.
![[Pasted image 20260920233952.png]]
Basically the dark blue gear will spin the cyan gear which will the move the racks back and forward. These racks will move the lenses and the displays back and forwards simultaneously to adjust to any person's eyes.

After finishing the axel I did some work on pins for the racks so they can actually move left and right. These are just a concept and will be measured and modified later in the project.
